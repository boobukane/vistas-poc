Study @ VISTAS – Exchange Programme

VISTAS welcomes students from partner overseas institutions for short-term study of one or two semesters. [Term dates, eligibility, credit limits and assessment details: to be confirmed by VISTAS]

The application steps

  1. Select the courses you wish to pursue at VISTAS [course list to be confirmed].
  2. Complete the Learning Agreement and have it signed by the authorised official at your home institution. If courses change after arrival, submit a revised Learning Agreement [deadline to be confirmed].
  3. Fill in the Application Form.
  4. Attach supporting documents: signed nomination form, passport copy, academic transcripts (in English), statement of purpose and letters of recommendation [final list to be confirmed].
  5. If you need accommodation, submit the Hostel Form.

Contact: International Affairs Office, VISTAS, Velan Nagar, P.V. Vaithiyalingam Road, Pallavaram · director.ia@vistas.ac.in

Study Abroad – Semester Exchange

VISTAS students may spend a semester or a year at a partner university overseas, with credits transferred on return. [Eligibility, credit transfer rules, funding and partner list: to be confirmed by VISTAS]

How to apply

  1. Check the partner institutions listed under Global Partnership and discuss options with your Head of Department.
  2. Prepare the Learning Agreement listing the courses to be studied abroad.
  3. Submit the Application Form to the International Affairs Office.
  4. Await nomination and the host university’s acceptance [process to be confirmed].

Application Form, Learning Agreement and Hostel Form are provided for inbound students only.
