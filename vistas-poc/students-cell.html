International Students Cell

VISTAS International Student Cell has been established to deal with the academic processes and directions to the concerns of international students and to seize the best of the opportunities. The cell enhances visibility of international initiatives such as studying abroad, international co-ops, and University partnerships. Additionally, it fosters research collaborations on a global scale, promotes understanding of globalization’s effects, cultivates globally aware citizens, and fosters productive international connections among students, scholars, alumni, and the wider community.

The standard operating procedure of VISTAS international students cell shall

  1. Exemplify updated regulatory body guidelines for Admission of International Students periodically.
  2. Maintain details of International and NRI students admitted every year and coordinate with the FRRO (Foreigner Regional and Registration Office) for S-form submission.
  3. Regularly review and appraise the needs of international students concerning the presence and functioning of the cell
  4. Conduct meetings to address the concerns of international students and resolve them.
  5. Facilitate seamless orientation of international students to campus facilities and events.
  6. Familiarize students with hostel rules, dress code, anti-ragging, local laws, customs, language, and civic amenities.
  7. Members of ISC take charge to resolve the queries raised by FRRO, Chennai.
  8. Motivating students to participate in curricular and extra-curricular activities such as games and sports, cultural presentations and social service activities.
  9. Organizing meetings for all foreign students to enable them to meet one another, exchange ideas and plan special activities.

International Student Services

Visa Information & Support

International students planning to study in India must obtain a Student Visa (S Visa) before traveling. The visa should be issued for the duration of the academic programme or as permitted by the Government of India. Students are advised to apply well in advance and ensure that their passport remains valid throughout their period of study.

Official Link
India Visa Online Portal: https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/
Student Visa Information: https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/visa

FRRO Registration

After arriving in India, eligible international students must complete FRRO/FRO registration within the prescribed time frame, as per Government of India regulations.

Official Link
FRRO e-FRRO Portal: https://indianfrro.gov.in/

Accommodation

VISTAS provides safe, secure, and comfortable on-campus accommodation for both international male and female students. Separate hostel facilities are available for boys and girls, offering a welcoming environment that supports academic success and personal well-being.

Accommodation Features:

  • Separate hostels for international boys and girls
  • Fully furnished rooms AC/ Non AC (single/shared occupancy, subject to availability)
  • 24×7 security with CCTV surveillance
  • Wi-Fi-enabled campus
  • Hygienic dining facilities serving nutritious meals
  • Housekeeping and laundry services
  • Medical assistance and emergency support
  • Easy access to academic buildings, library, sports facilities, and student amenities

Students are encouraged to apply for hostel accommodation soon after receiving their admission offer, as rooms are allotted based on availability. The International Relations Office assists international students with hostel allocation and provides support throughout their stay.

Scholarship for International / NRI / OCI / Foreign Students at VISTAS

At Vels Institute of Science, Technology & Advanced Studies (VISTAS), we are committed to promoting global education by supporting meritorious students from diverse international backgrounds. To encourage academic excellence and attract talented learners worldwide, VISTAS offers tuition fee scholarships for NRI, OCI, and Foreign National students admitted to various programmes of the University.

Scholarships are awarded based on the marks obtained in Grade 12 / equivalent qualifying examination, helping deserving students access quality higher education in India.

Orientation Programme

An orientation programme for all International Students is organised at the start of the academic year to facilitate the smooth academic, social, cultural, and personal transition of newly admitted international students into the university community. The programme familiarizes students with the institution’s academic systems, campus facilities, regulations, student support services, and Indian culture, ensuring that they feel welcomed, informed, and prepared for a successful academic journey.
